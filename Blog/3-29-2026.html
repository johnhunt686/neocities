The blog is born.

A short summary of my thoughts on blogging, and the methodology I intend to implement.

Structure and Order

I quite admire the simplicity and power of delivering text in authentic html. Many modern approches just spam divs and assign properties, I dispise this approach as it obscures information massively. The ability to scrape and interact with the web in a predictable manner is a lost art. Imagine with me, for a momment, you are dissatisfied with the sites you like to read from. So, like any capabale person with access to a computer, you look to change this. You write a simple tool to scrape your sites every day and pick out the important bits, headers, content, asides, quotes, all styled to your liking. One could, for instance, format all sites in their favorite, readable style.
